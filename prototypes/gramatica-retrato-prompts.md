# Il ritratto: prompts das 5 imagens

A imagem é sempre **o quadro**, não o Matteo. Ele não envelhece; quem carrega o preço é a pintura.

Como gerar no GPT:
1. Gere a imagem 1 com o prompt base mais o prompt do bloco 1.
2. Nas imagens de 2 a 5, anexe a imagem anterior e peça: "Edit this exact painting, same man, same pose, same framing, same canvas and lighting. Change only: …" seguido do texto do bloco.

Formato 4:5 vertical (1024×1280). Sem moldura (o app desenha a moldura). Sem texto.

## Prompt base (vai junto com o da imagem 1)

> Oil painting on canvas, 4:5 vertical, half-length portrait of a 20-year-old Italian man, slim, dark wavy hair falling over his forehead, pale olive skin, dark eyes, a simple dark wool coat over a white shirt with an open collar. Three-quarter view, looking just past the viewer. Background: a dim Venetian painter's studio in winter, a tall window with grey lagoon light on the left, the rest dissolving into warm brown shadow. Visible brushstrokes, Venetian Renaissance palette (umber, ochre, muted crimson, deep green), chiaroscuro, a slight craquelure texture. Painterly and literary in mood, not photographic. No frame, no text, no signature.

## Bloco 1 · Fundamentos: "Prima del desiderio"

> He looks young, hopeful and a little poor: the coat is worn at the cuffs, cheeks slightly hollow, an uncertain half-smile, eyes bright and open. The light from the window falls softly on his face. Overall feeling: innocence, waiting for life to begin.

## Bloco 2 · Estrutura das frases: "Il primo applauso"

> Change only: the coat is now new and well cut, a dark silk cravat at the neck. His face is more beautiful, the skin flawless. The smile has become self-assured, the chin a little higher. In the eyes, for the first time, a faint coldness. A single wilted red rose lies on the sill behind him.

## Bloco 3 · Conjugações: "Che cosa ho fatto?"

> Change only: the smile is now thin and cruel at one corner of the mouth. The eyes have darkened and look harder, almost contemptuous. Faint lines around the mouth the real man doesn't have. The window light is colder and greyer, the shadows behind him deeper. The craquelure has spread across the lower part of the canvas.

## Bloco 4 · Conectivos: "Le maschere"

> Change only: the face has aged about twenty years in the paint: sunken cheeks, heavy-lidded eyes, a sly, calculating expression. The beautiful clothes look the same, which makes the face more disturbing. Behind him, in the shadow, a half-visible Venetian carnival mask hangs on the wall. The varnish has yellowed and the paint is starting to crack around the eyes.

## Bloco 5 · Gramática intermediária: "Se non avessi desiderato"

> Change only: the face in the painting is old, grey and ravaged by everything he has done: deep creases, bloodless lips, eyes full of guilt and fear, looking straight at the viewer now. Still the same elegant coat. The studio behind him has gone almost black; only a sliver of cold light from the window remains. Heavy craquelure and darkened varnish, like a painting left in an attic for decades. Unsettling and tragic, not gory: no blood, no wounds, no gore.
